Founder

Ali Alkis

Nuclear Security Researcher & Policy Professional

Ali Alkis is a nuclear security researcher whose work focuses on nuclear security during armed conflict, nuclear terrorism, emerging technologies and international nuclear security governance.

He is a PhD candidate at Hacettepe University, where his doctoral research examines the protection of nuclear facilities during armed conflict and the evolving role of international institutions in nuclear security.

Ali is a Junior Associate Fellow at the NATO Defense College, where he engages with research and policy communities working on international security.

His research interests include nuclear security in wartime, the protection of nuclear facilities during armed conflict, nuclear terrorism, radiological security, artificial intelligence, emerging technologies, nuclear nonproliferation and strategic risk.

His research and commentary have appeared in academic, policy and international security publications, including the Bulletin of the Atomic Scientists, the Journal of Nuclear Sciences, the International Journal of Nuclear Security and other policy-oriented outlets.

Research perspective

Beyond traditional threat assumptions.

Beyond DBT grew out of Ali Alkis's research into the changing nature of nuclear security risks and the limitations of traditional threat assumptions.


His work particularly examines what happens when nuclear facilities, materials and infrastructure become exposed to conditions that were not anticipated when conventional security assumptions were developed.


This includes the intersection of nuclear security with armed conflict, terrorism, emerging technologies, artificial intelligence and strategic competition.
