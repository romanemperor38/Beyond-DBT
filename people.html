Founder

Ali Alkis

Nuclear Security Researcher & Policy Professional

Ali Alkis is a nuclear security researcher whose work focuses on emerging nuclear security challenges at the intersection of armed conflict, nuclear terrorism, international security and emerging technologies.

He is a PhD candidate at Hacettepe University, where his doctoral research examines the protection of nuclear facilities during armed conflict and the evolving role of international institutions in nuclear security.

Ali is also a Junior Associate Fellow at the NATO Defense College, where he engages with research and policy communities working on international security.

His research interests include nuclear security during armed conflict, the protection of nuclear facilities, nuclear terrorism, radiological security, artificial intelligence, emerging technologies, nuclear nonproliferation and international nuclear security governance.

His research and commentary have appeared in academic journals, international policy publications and research institutions, including the Bulletin of the Atomic Scientists, the Stimson Center, the International Journal of Nuclear Security and other specialist publications.

Research philosophy

Beyond the traditional boundaries.

Nuclear security increasingly sits at the intersection of technology, geopolitics, armed conflict and international governance.


Beyond DBT therefore approaches nuclear security as a multidisciplinary field rather than a narrowly technical one.


The initiative seeks to connect academic research with practical security questions and to encourage dialogue between communities that do not always interact.
