Founder

Ali Alkis

Nuclear Security Researcher & Policy Professional

Ali Alkis is a nuclear security researcher and policy professional whose work focuses on nuclear security during armed conflict, nuclear terrorism, emerging technologies, and international nuclear security governance.

He is a PhD candidate at Hacettepe University, where his doctoral research examines the protection of nuclear facilities during armed conflict and the evolving role of international institutions in nuclear security.

Ali is also a Junior Associate Fellow at the NATO Defense College, where he engages with research and policy communities working on international security.

His research interests include nuclear security in wartime Ukraine, artificial intelligence and nuclear security, radiological terrorism, nuclear nonproliferation, strategic risk, and the evolution of the Design Basis Threat.

His work has been presented through international nuclear security and strategic-policy forums and published in academic and policy-oriented outlets.

Research perspective

Beyond traditional threat assumptions.

Beyond DBT was developed around a simple observation: the nuclear security environment is changing faster than many traditional security assumptions.


The initiative brings together research on nuclear security, strategic affairs, emerging technology and international governance to explore what security should look like when the threat extends beyond the baseline.
