About Beyond DBT

Beyond the baseline.

An independent research and policy initiative examining how nuclear security can adapt to an evolving threat environment.

Our mission

Anticipate. Strengthen. Connect.

Beyond DBT is an independent research and policy initiative focused on emerging nuclear and radiological security challenges.


The initiative examines threats and vulnerabilities that may fall outside traditional nuclear security assumptions, bringing together academic research, policy analysis and professional dialogue.


Our objective is to contribute to a nuclear security community that is better prepared for uncertainty, technological change and complex security environments.

Why Beyond DBT?

The threat environment is evolving.

The Design Basis Threat has long provided an important foundation for nuclear security planning.


It establishes assumptions about the adversaries, capabilities and scenarios that security systems should be designed to withstand.


But contemporary security challenges increasingly cross traditional boundaries.


Armed conflict, artificial intelligence, cyber operations, autonomous systems, unconventional actors and rapidly evolving technologies can create threats that are difficult to capture within static assumptions.


Beyond DBT asks what nuclear security should look like when the threat exceeds the baseline.

Our principles

Independent. Evidence-based. Forward-looking.

01

Independence

Research and analysis are developed independently and are not intended to represent the views of any government, institution or organization.

02

Evidence

We prioritize rigorous research, credible evidence and careful analysis over assumptions or political narratives.

03

International Perspective

Nuclear security challenges do not stop at national borders. Our research considers perspectives from across the international community.

04

Future Orientation

We focus not only on today's threats, but also on capabilities and scenarios that may shape tomorrow's security environment.

Leadership

Built around research.

Beyond DBT was founded by nuclear security researcher Ali Alkis.

Ali's research focuses on nuclear security during armed conflict, nuclear terrorism, emerging technologies, international nuclear security governance and the evolution of traditional nuclear security concepts.


His academic and policy work has appeared in international academic journals, policy publications and research institutions.

Institutional independence

The initiative seeks constructive engagement across academic, governmental, industry and civil-society communities while maintaining analytical independence.
