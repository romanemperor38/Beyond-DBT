About Beyond DBT

Beyond the baseline.

An independent research and policy initiative focused on the future of nuclear security.

Our mission

Anticipate. Strengthen. Connect.

Beyond DBT is an independent research and policy initiative examining emerging nuclear and radiological security challenges that extend beyond traditional threat assumptions.


The initiative brings together research, policy analysis, professional exchange and educational activities to explore how nuclear security can adapt to a rapidly changing strategic and technological environment.

Why Beyond DBT?

The threat environment is evolving.


The Design Basis Threat has long provided an important foundation for nuclear security planning.


Yet contemporary security challenges increasingly cross traditional boundaries.


Armed conflict, artificial intelligence, autonomous systems, cyber operations, unconventional actors and rapidly evolving technologies can create threats that are difficult to capture within static assumptions.


Beyond DBT explores how nuclear security can remain resilient when the threat exceeds the baseline.
