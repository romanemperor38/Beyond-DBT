Selected work

Research across disciplines.

Ali Alkis's research examines nuclear security, nuclear terrorism, armed conflict, nonproliferation, emerging technologies and international security governance.


The publications below include peer-reviewed academic research, policy analysis, institutional publications and commentary. They reflect an evolving research agenda focused on understanding how nuclear security can adapt to changing threat environments.
