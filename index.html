Independent Nuclear Security Initiative

The threat is changing. Nuclear security must change with it.

Beyond DBT is an independent research and policy initiative examining emerging nuclear security challenges at the intersection of armed conflict, terrorism, technology and strategic risk.

Explore our research
Why Beyond DBT

Security assumptions cannot remain static.

Nuclear security systems are built around defined assumptions about the threats they are expected to address.


Yet today's security environment is increasingly shaped by armed conflict, emerging technologies, artificial intelligence, cyber operations, unconventional actors and rapidly evolving strategic risks.


Beyond DBT examines what happens when the threat exceeds the baseline.
